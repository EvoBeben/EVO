Backdrop

Backdrop

what set the tone

The war trade unwound

The US and Iran paused strikes over the weekend to make space for diplomacy, and Iran said it would hold off while the pause holds. Brent fell 8.7% to $88.36 and WTI 7.5% to $82.61. Caspian Pipeline loadings resumed, adding supply back.

The Fed on Wednesday

A hold at 3.50–3.75% is priced at roughly 62–65%, so a hike is a live 35–38% tail. September is at 82%. Warsh has told Congress the committee has “no tolerance for persistently elevated inflation” and has dropped forward guidance entirely.

China moved the chips

Changxin Technology’s Shanghai listing landed, and a state-backed Shanghai firm was reported to have begun mass production of immersion DUV lithography. ASML fell as much as 8%, memory names 6–11%. The SOX is down roughly 18–19% from its late-June high.

The capex question moves on

Alphabet beat on every line and fell 7.1% for guiding FY26 capex to $195–205bn. Microsoft, Meta and Amazon now report inside 48 hours. Meta’s guide of $125–145bn and its 41.4% Q1 operating margin are the numbers to watch.

A split tape

Monday split cleanly: real estate, materials, consumer defensive and communication services rose, while energy and technology fell. The Dow made the gain, the Nasdaq did not — Nvidia alone took 5% off the index’s biggest weight.

Still to come

Four sessions remain. Fed Wednesday 2pm, then Microsoft, Meta, Qualcomm and Boeing; Apple and Amazon plus Core PCE on Thursday; Exxon and Chevron Friday into a crude price that has round-tripped a fortnight of war premium.
